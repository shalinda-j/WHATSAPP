## 2025-02-18 - Webhook Management IDOR & Mass Assignment
**Vulnerability:** The webhooks management endpoints (`PUT /:id`, `DELETE /:id`, `POST /:id/test`) were using unscoped `findById...` methods, allowing users to modify, delete, or trigger webhooks belonging to other tenants. Additionally, `PUT /:id` blindly merged `req.body` via `findByIdAndUpdate`, creating a mass assignment risk where restricted fields (like `tenantId`) could be overwritten.
**Learning:** Even when the listing route `GET /` correctly applies `withTenantScope`, individual document routes frequently miss the scope check when relying on `req.params.id`.
**Prevention:** Always use `findOne...` combined with `withTenantScope(req, { _id: req.params.id })` for operations on tenant-scoped documents. Never pass `req.body` directly to update functions; always filter for explicitly allowed fields.
